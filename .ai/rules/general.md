# General

## Keep executor returns owner-directed
Follow the owner hands-on review, refinement and explicit report-permission sequence in TALA-Orchestrator-Protocol.md Section 1. Local readiness stays in the executor chat; it triggers no automatic message to the orchestrator. Only after the owner-permitted consolidated return does the orchestrator review and hand remediation back. Section 8 governs case-specific authorized Issue/PRD updates by the orchestrator, executor or bounded helpers.

## Use the canonical UI brief lifecycle
For UI-bearing child planning, recording, execution, and review, follow 00_Project_Documents/TALA-Orchestrator-Protocol.md: its UI-Bearing Slice Completeness Checklist and existing execution, coordination/handoff, and verification steps.
The protocol owns brief reuse, material-decision approval, authorized persistence, and handoff/review requirements; installed Impeccable instructions own the method. Backend-only children need no UI brief. Preserve existing permission boundaries.

## Keep Issue criteria practical
Follow the Orchestrator Protocol's Practical acceptance criteria section and Section 7 when deriving or verifying Issues. Keep outcomes understandable, bounded and feasible to prove; reuse sufficient evidence and stop adding checks once owned requirements are verified. Section 1 preserves owner/user/client judgment of the actual experience.

## Keep TALA governance outside Boost-generated blocks
Boost owns generated `<laravel-boost-guidelines>` blocks and generated skills. Routine SDLC edits in `AGENTS.md` are limited to `<TALA_ORCHESTRATOR_ROUTER>`; leave other generated agent files unchanged. The TALA Orchestrator Protocol owns accepted-contract reuse, task-specific readiness, and continuation to the explicitly authorized endpoint; companions link to it rather than introduce extra approval rounds.
