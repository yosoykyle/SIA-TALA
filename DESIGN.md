# Design System: Servitech Institute Asia — TALA

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Clear Institutional Operations"**

This is a refinement of TALA's recognizable blue-led institutional interface, not a replacement theme. The September 25, 2026 user correction supersedes Candidate #6's archival-navy/parchment visual world while preserving the school-first product truth and the approved Impeccable-led UX process. Servitech Institute Asia Inc. leads; TALA appears secondarily as “Powered by TALA.”

The existing palette, typography, crest, and TALA mark are continuity anchors. Existing page compositions, controls, and workflow friction are not implicitly approved. Each operating surface should make its real task, current state, responsible owner, primary action, and recovery path easy to understand. Impeccable-led work may substantially reorganize a screen or use a focused custom visualization when stock components obscure that task.

**Key characteristics:**
- Recognizable institutional blue, light surfaces, restrained yellow accent, and full-color marks on screen.
- Clear hierarchy and task-oriented language across public, Applicant, Student, Faculty, and Staff experiences.
- Operational layouts suited to the information being used, not a universal dense-table or card pattern.
- Formal, version-bound, monochrome-capable official outputs with the school as issuer.

## Colors

The established light palette is recorded in the canonical UI Surface Blueprint and current shared CSS, rather than newly selected by this seed:

- **Institutional blue** (#1D4ED8): primary actions, selected navigation, and links on suitable light surfaces.
- **Institutional deep blue** (#1E3A8A): stronger brand surfaces and emphasis.
- **Restrained yellow** (#FACC15): small brand cue or accent with sufficient foreground contrast; not a broad navigation stripe.
- **Cool light canvas** (#F8FAFC) and **white surface** (#FFFFFF): page ground and task containers.
- **Primary ink** (#0F172A), **muted ink** (#475569), and **structural border** (#CBD5E1): readable hierarchy and boundaries.

Semantic success, warning, danger, information, focus, and Dark/System variants follow the UI Blueprint and actual accessible implementation. Their final extracted component tokens remain [to be resolved from implemented UI]. Do not substitute the former parchment ground or archival palette as a new default.

**The school-first color rule.** Blue supports the institution's interface; a TALA mark or yellow accent never displaces the school identity or the user's task.

## Typography

Retain the established Outfit heading and Inter interface/body families with system fallbacks, as governed by the UI Blueprint and existing assets. Numeric records, schedules, grades, and money benefit from tabular figures. Exact type sizes, weights, and component-specific treatments remain [to be verified and extracted from the refined implementation]; this seed does not impose a new scale.

## Layout

Public arrival, guided learner tasks, operational workbenches, and official outputs may need different compositions within one visual identity. Put the current context and next meaningful action where the user can find them; progressively disclose secondary evidence and exceptional controls. Keep responsive reading order and accessible alternatives. A visual weekly timetable may be the primary scheduling view when that best supports review, paired with the equivalent filterable meeting table required by the UI Blueprint. A stock table or modal is not mandatory merely because it is native to Filament.

Specific split ratios, navigation dimensions, and screen compositions belong to approved surface briefs and verified implementation, not this global seed.

## Elevation & Depth

Use the existing contrast between blue brand regions, light canvas, white task surfaces, and clear boundaries. Reserve elevation for a real hierarchy or active layer; do not introduce parchment texture, ledger ornament, or decorative shadows as a system-wide requirement. Exact elevation tokens remain [to be extracted from implemented UI].

## Shapes

Keep the familiar, moderately rounded control and container language unless a surface-specific usability reason calls for change. Consistent boundaries and recognizable control affordances matter more than an archival metaphor. Exact radius and border tokens remain [to be extracted from implemented UI].

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Lead visible school services and official documents with Servitech Institute Asia Inc.; keep “Powered by TALA” secondary.
- Refine the established blue-led identity while redesigning confusing workflows, hierarchy, and components where evidence warrants it.
- Preserve native Light/Dark/System behavior, readable contrast, visible focus, and meaning that does not depend on color alone.
- Choose a component for the user's task; use focused custom UI within Laravel/Filament when native presentation is inadequate.

### Don't
- Treat Candidate #6's archival palette, parchment material, or ledger motifs as active visual authority.
- Treat historical prototype layouts or today's production screens as automatically approved compositions.
- Force scheduling into a table-only view, or use a visual grid without an accessible equivalent.
- Add decorative density, redundant cards, or arbitrary new theme elements that slow ordinary tasks.
