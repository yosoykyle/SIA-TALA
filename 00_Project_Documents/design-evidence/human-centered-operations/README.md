## QA summary and known limits

The external `design-qa.md` records historical build/browser checks and a passing representative-prototype assessment. That narrative covers several refinement stages; it does not make every older PNG the final accepted state. Its old statement that hero/Program/map additions require authority correction predates the accepted targeted correction now present in PRD 01 and the blueprint. It is not a current exclusion.

This refresh verified capture identity/state, image readability, dimensions, hashes and preservation of source/history. It did not rerun production acceptance, a comprehensive prototype accessibility audit, build/tests, provider verification or print issuance.

Known interpretation limits:

- The context menu contains Applicant/Student/Staff labels in its DOM, but the prototype renders their role headings with inadequate contrast on the white dropdown. The capture preserves that limitation; production must provide readable names and required contrast.
- Representative navigation includes labels/groupings that differ from the canonical role map. Current Student data, balances, progress, dates, admission status and operational status are design fixtures, not records or rules.
- The map is a live embedded third-party view. Its visible labels can change and do not approve an institutional address, provider setup or availability claim. Use current configured, institution-approved location information and the canonical external-link fallback.
- Portrait/landscape captures show representative paper hierarchy and status distinction. Sample locations, identifiers, “official” labels and “Page 1 of 1” do not establish factual institutional data, issuance, orientation for every document or working multipage numbering. Canonical print/output contracts govern.
- Light-only prototype rendering and mock auth buttons are limitations of the evidence, not permission to remove native production capability. A screenshot cannot prove server authorization, safe publication, persistence or recovery.
